Zero EMU

为双屏折叠 iPhone 设计的掌机模拟器：展开就是两块屏幕，合上盖子游戏自动休眠，打开接着玩。普通 iPhone 也能用。

A dual-screen handheld emulator designed for the foldable iPhone. Open it and the two halves are the two screens; close the lid to sleep, open to play on. Works on any iPhone.
