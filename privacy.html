隐私政策

Zero EMU 不收集任何个人数据。没有账户、没有统计、没有广告，开发者没有自己的服务器，App 不会把你的任何内容发给我们。

留在你设备上的内容

你导入的文件、存档、即时存档、设置、游戏笔记、掌机里的"用户信息"（昵称、生日、国家或地区，只用来在掌机里显示），以及用掌机「相机」拍的照片和录像，都只保存在这台设备上。其中模拟机的存储在「文件」App 的"我的 iPhone › Zero EMU"里看得到；设备备份会包含这些内容。

相机和照片

只有在你打开掌机里的「相机」时才使用摄像头。照片和录像存在本机，并另存一份到「照片」。Zero EMU 只向「照片」添加，不读取你的相册。

麦克风

只在相机的「闪闪」、麦克风测试和需要吹气的软件运行时使用，用来判断有没有在吹气，不录音。相机录像时录下的声音只存在录像文件里。

iCloud 存档同步（可选）

默认关闭。打开后，存档会保存到你自己的 iCloud（或你选的文件夹），由 Apple 按你的 Apple 账户处理。开发者看不到这些文件。

App 内购买

购买和订阅由 Apple 处理。我们收不到你的姓名、账户或付款信息，App 只知道这台设备上是否已经购买。

本地网络

在 Zero EMU 里运行的软件可以像在原主机上一样使用网络，例如自制软件通过局域网接收电脑发来的程序。这些连接由你运行的软件发起，Zero EMU 不记录也不上传其中的任何内容。第一次有软件使用局域网时，系统会请你允许"本地网络"权限。

诊断

如果你在 iOS 设置里同意与 App 开发者共享分析数据，Apple 会向我们提供不含个人身份的崩溃和性能统计。App 里的"导出日志"只在你自己操作时生成文件，发给谁由你决定。

Privacy Policy

Zero EMU does not collect any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics and no ads; the developer runs no server, and the app sends nothing of yours to us.

What stays on your device

Files you import, save data, save states, settings, game notes, the console's user profile (nickname, birthday, country or region — only shown inside the console) and the photos and videos taken with the console's Camera are stored on this device only. The emulated system's storage is visible in the Files app under On My iPhone › Zero EMU; device backups include this content.

Camera and Photos

The camera is used only while you have the console's Camera open. Photos and videos are stored on the device and a copy is saved to Photos. Zero EMU only adds to Photos; it does not read your photo library.

Microphone

Used only by the Camera's Sparkle lens, the microphone test and software that asks you to blow, to tell whether you are blowing; nothing is recorded. Sound recorded with a Camera video is kept in that video file only.

iCloud save sync (optional)

Off by default. When you turn it on, saves are stored in your own iCloud (or a folder you choose) and handled by Apple under your Apple Account. The developer cannot see these files.

In-app purchases

Purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple. We receive no name, account or payment details; the app only knows whether a purchase is active on this device.

Local network

Software you run inside Zero EMU can use the network as it would on the original hardware — for example, homebrew tools that receive programs from a computer on the local network. Such connections are made by the software you run; Zero EMU does not log or upload any of their content. The first time software uses the local network, the system asks you to allow Local Network access.

Diagnostics

If you have agreed in iOS Settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple provides us with crash and performance statistics that do not identify you. The app's Export log makes a file only when you ask for it, and you decide who receives it.
